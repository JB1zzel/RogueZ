@in_help
Rogue 5.4.4 by Michael Toy, Ken Arnold and Glenn Wichman. Find the Amulet of Yendor (below level 26) and bring it back to the surface. The Info topic of the MENU tells you how to play.
.
Z88 version by Jamie Bradbury, 2026.

@hlp_info
Moving, fighting, things, the screen and saving.

@hlp_game
Save, see the whole level, redraw or quit.

@hlp_opts
Your name, for the tombstone.

@hlp_aim
Rooms are joined by passages. Find the stairs (%) and press > to go down. Gold, potions, scrolls, rings, wands, armour and weapons lie about; monsters (letters) get stronger deeper down. With the Amulet, < goes back up.

@hlp_moving
Cursor keys or h j k l (y u b n diagonally) move. {SHIFT} and a direction runs; {DIAMOND} and a cursor key runs until something turns up. s searches for hidden doors and traps; > and < use stairs.

@hlp_fighting
Walk into a monster to hit it. F and a direction fights to the death; t throws, z zaps a wand. Rest (.) to heal.

@hlp_items
i lists what you carry. q quaff, r read, e eat, w wield, W wear, T take off armour, P put on, R remove a ring, d drop, c call (name) a thing.

@hlp_more
/ names a symbol. m and a direction moves without picking up; ^ and a direction names a trap. , picks up, I shows one thing, a repeats a command. ) ] = @ show weapon, armour, rings, state. D lists what you know. {DIAMOND}R redraws, {DIAMOND}P repeats the last message, ESC cancels.

@hlp_screen
Messages are on the top line; the map below follows you; your state is on the right. M (or {DIAMOND}L) shows the whole level, 7 rows at a time (j and k move).

@hlp_saving
S (or {DIAMOND}S) saves to rogue.sav and play goes on. Rogue offers the saved game when it starts; once restored, the file is deleted. After you die, a new game starts.

@hlp_redraw
Draw the screen again.

@hlp_quit
Give up this game (Rogue asks first). The application closes.

@hlp_o_name
Type your name. DEL rubs out, ENTER finishes, ESC keeps the old one.
