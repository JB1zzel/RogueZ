@in_help
Rogue 5.4.4: Exploring the Dungeons of Doom, by Michael Toy, Ken Arnold and Glenn Wichman.
.
Go down through the levels of the dungeon, find the Amulet of Yendor (below level 26) and bring it back up to the surface.
.
The Info topic of the MENU tells you how to play.

@hlp_info
How to move, fight and use things, what the screen shows, and saving games.

@hlp_game
Save the game, look at the whole level, redraw the screen or quit.

@hlp_opts
Your name, for the tombstone.

@hlp_aim
Each level is a set of rooms joined by passages. Find the stairs (%) and press > to go down. Gold, potions, scrolls, rings, wands, armour and weapons lie about; monsters (letters) grow stronger the deeper you go.
.
With the Amulet, < takes you back up.

@hlp_moving
Cursor keys or h j k l (and y u b n for diagonals) move one step. SHIFT with a direction runs; DIAMOND with a cursor key runs until something turns up.
.
s searches for hidden doors and traps. > and < use stairs.

@hlp_fighting
Walk into a monster to attack it. F and a direction fights to the death. t throws, z zaps a wand. Rest (.) to get hit points back.

@hlp_items
i lists what you carry. q quaff, r read, e eat, w wield, W wear armour, T take it off, P put on a ring, R remove it, d drop, c call (name) a thing. ? lists every command.

@hlp_screen
The top line has the messages; the map rows below it follow you around the level. Your state is on the right. Walls are drawn as lines and passages shaded.
.
M (or DIAMOND L) shows the rest of the level, 7 rows at a time.

@hlp_saving
Save (DIAMOND S, or S) writes the game to rogue.sav and play goes on. When Rogue starts it offers the saved game; once restored, the file is deleted.
.
After you die, a new game starts.

@hlp_save
Save the game in rogue.sav. It is offered when Rogue starts again.

@hlp_map
Show the whole level, 7 rows at a time (j and k move the view).

@hlp_redraw
Draw the screen again.

@hlp_quit
Give up this game (Rogue asks first). The application closes.

@hlp_o_name
Type the name for the tombstone. DEL rubs out, ENTER finishes, ESC keeps the old one.
